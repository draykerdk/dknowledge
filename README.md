> Knowledge with sources, context and a path to correction.

Dknowledge is Drayker's layer of knowledge, memory and operational ontology, repeated at personal, local and global scales. It preserves provenance, relations, lines of decision and access permissions so that the knowledge used by the Dk can be reconstructed and audited at the appropriate level. Today it connects the papers, architecture, decisions and evidence of Drayker; its longer-term design links public, project and personal knowledge under distinct access and provenance rules.

Knowledge records should retain their origin, scope, revision history and relevant dependencies, so that a correction can prompt review of the conclusions built on it.

Shared memory becomes more useful when people can inspect how it was formed, challenge it and carry corrections forward.

## A practical example

When a measurement is corrected, a project should be able to locate the analysis and decision that used it and record whether their conclusions still hold. This is an illustration of the proposed design.

## Why this exists

Dknowledge is the memory of it: what has been decided, tried, learned and discarded, still attached to its sources.

The argument in full is on the [manifesto](https://drayker.org/manifesto/); the [economy page](https://drayker.org/economy/) states plainly what contributing here earns and what it does not.

## What it becomes

The systemic final form of Dknowledge is described as **evolutionary knowledge graphs** — a version of the knowledge layer that is more than a graph of linked documents, and more complex than a wiki can be. Requirements, decisions and evidence stay connected by trust derived from evidence rather than from position; the graph evolves with the system, so the memory of the network is not a snapshot but a living structure — the same fractal logic [Dk](https://dk.drayker.org) is designed around, applied to knowledge itself. The central name for that trust is **contextual reputation**, the evolving form of the network's metadata, present in almost everything: data, relations, functions and members. Each datum and each relation carries levels of trust and metadata that evolve with use and with results: its reputation grows as it proves valid against other information and in the real world, and falls when the world contradicts it, while the metadata keep its origin, its context and the path that led to its current standing. It can measure trust, integration, validation in reality, contribution or use, and each type has its own evaluation rules. The same logic applies to UIDs; it is one of the foundations of the network and ties together almost everything in the architecture, in governance, in the distribution of resources and in external relations, and the general reputation of members is one of its cases. Today Dknowledge is pages, papers and roadmaps connected to their sources. What it becomes is a structure that only exists as the whole.

Each scale of the intelligence carries its own knowledge: the personal Dk is connected to the person's personal Dknowledge, the global Dk to global Dknowledge, and every organization and project has its own Dknowledge in connection with a specialized Dk. Dknowledge is not one repository of everything — it is the pattern repeated at every scale, and the global Dknowledge is the junction of them all. At the global scale, kept in harmony by the global Dk, it is the network's world model, and the maturity of each Dknowledge, personal or of a project, is what weighs most in the precision of the Dk that uses it.

One image helped design the circulation between what is in use and what stays available outside the focus. The active context of a Dk plays the part of the conscious mind, personal Dknowledge that of the personal unconscious, the shared state the global Dk weighs that of the collective conscious, and the rules, protocols and weights that orient the whole before any choice that of the collective unconscious. In the psyche the unconscious works unseen; in the network it stays open to examination. The E.C.H. triad describes this circulation: the new comes in through what is in use, its relations thicken in memory, and harmony checks the result against the rules of the whole, or reorganizes what was already known when the contradiction is real.

One of the conceptual bases for that pattern is the **E.C.H. triad: Expansion, Complexity and Harmony**, a metabolism of the three movements by which a symbolic network changes. Expansion brings in experience, data and knowledge, without treating accumulation as the goal. Complexity thickens useful relations among elements, connecting entries to sources, decisions, hypotheses and other relations. Harmony resolves dissonance and stabilizes the network, fitting in the new, draining the force from what cannot hold up, or rebuilding what existed, and it can keep the truth or protect an error. In Dknowledge, it seeks coherence, authenticity and integrity without erasing relevant contradictions: it may integrate a new entry, keep alternatives contextualized, or conclude that the previous state must be reconstructed. Because the network is almost fractal, the cycle repeats at personal, project and global scales.

Learning crosses scales in two distinct ways. In **anonymous federated learning**, a design requirement for combining distributed learning with the protection of identity and personal context, each experience stays where it was lived, and what circulates, with authorization, are nameless patterns or model updates with depersonalized metadata. Global Dknowledge can therefore hold broader context and deeper learned structure without becoming a collection of personal histories; when a pattern is validated independently in other instances, its weight in global learning becomes progressively stronger. In **deliberate contribution**, a person or unit chooses to share knowledge, evidence or narrative under explicit permissions and provenance. The system sees only the sum of many contributions, never that of one person, and each pattern is weighed by what it allows one to guess, because even a nameless pattern can point to someone. These protections are checked in the results, and not only in the design. Neither route makes private context common property, and the federated route still requires a re-identification threat model before implementation.

Importantly, **memory is corrigible and contestable**. An individual member is designed to retain sovereign authority to inspect, contest, and correct algorithmic inferences made by their agent, revoking associations or exporting their relational memory graph without platform lock-in. 

Dknowledge inverts the legibility that has accompanied power. To govern, states made people legible, and platforms carried that legibility into intimacy, while the rules and models of those who govern stayed opaque to those who obey. In Drayker transparency runs the other way: the member's life stays protected at the edge, and the decisions, rules and allocations above have to show where they came from. When a person chooses to open more of their context to their own Dk, that legibility strengthens their representation, not anyone's power over them.

Dknowledge is also designed as Dk's oracle. For each problem Dk solves, it will record the process and explain it in two registers, a didactic one and a technical one, so the knowledge can be studied in the Academy and reviewed by Dk itself as an intelligent memory. A ledger of function responses will return results already computed instead of processing them again.

Dk and Dknowledge remain distinct. Dk coordinates, reasons and proposes actions over the available context. Dknowledge preserves the evolving state, provenance, permissions and decision lines needed to reconstruct what the system knew and why it changed. Epistemologically, Dknowledge operates under the core Drayker principle: **kind to people, relentless with ideas** — protecting the unconditional dignity and situated context of every human participant, while holding every technical hypothesis, proof, and allocation to uncompromising empirical verification. Economic models documented here keep one ledger in three parts: common reserves, project custody and personal balances are recorded separately; only the personal part is counted in Dktron, and inactivity alone does not cause its forfeiture.

## How it fits the whole

Dknowledge is the connective tissue: [Dk](https://dk.drayker.org) is the intelligence, [DFM](https://dfmp.drayker.org) is the method, [PAP](https://pap.drayker.org) is the durable environment for projects and applications, and [DAF](https://daf.drayker.org) is an autonomous, distributed federation of autonomous units, the groups and organizations of people working on different questions and projects in Drayker, the first phase in the distribution of the Drayker Embassy's power and a basic, primitive form of PAP, implemented now to test its dynamics in the construction itself — and this is where what they mean, what is being designed and what was decided stay traceable to their sources.

A complex system needs memory that can be *followed*, not just information that can be stored. That is the whole reason this repository exists.

It is the memory the rest of the ecosystem reads from and writes to. The [Dk](https://dk.drayker.org) intelligence draws on it for architecture and decisions. Papers and resolutions from [DFMP](https://dfmp.drayker.org) are documented here. The [Academy](https://academy.drayker.org) reads from it and feeds learning back. Evidence from [open science](https://science.drayker.org) and projects in transition from [Emergence](https://ei.drayker.org) land here. Even the historical roadmaps are kept — dated, and separated from current work — because the reasoning is worth reading even when the phase has passed.
