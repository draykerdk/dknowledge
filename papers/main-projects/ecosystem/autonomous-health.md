# Autonomous Health

This is the current public formulation of Autonomous Health in relation to the whole Drayker system. Nothing described here operates today.

## Systemic role

Autonomous Health is the health system of Drayker: an artificial intelligence health system integrated with each member's [UID](https://uid.drayker.org), [Dk Personal](https://personal.drayker.org), personal [Dknowledge](https://dknowledge.drayker.org) and devices. Its purpose is that a person can follow their own health, prevent disease and receive accompanied, optimised and personalised treatment, so they can live longer and better, accessibly, with access to frontier research and discoveries.

For approved members, Autonomous Health is the form the common floor takes in health ([Distributed Support](./support-model.md)).

## Detection by patterns

The system relates what a person's Dk Personal, personal Dknowledge and devices already know about them. When a pattern may be connected to a condition with sufficient probability, it is investigated to find out whether something is there. Besides saving lives, every investigation improves the multifactor detection of every kind of condition, through patterns no one would have thought to look for, and makes it more precise over time.

The data belong to the member. What enters an investigation is what the member authorises their Dk Personal to share.

## Diagnosis and treatment

A diagnostic intelligence learns from every exam: the detailed exams of the clinics that use the system and large exam datasets gathered around the world. A treatment intelligence relates each diagnosis to the studies and articles on it, proposes the best possible solutions, and learns from their results and feedback. Clinics run with artificial intelligence, with professional review alongside AI diagnosis and monitoring.

Every clinic, hospital or organisation that uses the system has an identity, and every professional is registered. Data and processing are distributed across the network rather than held by one operator, and when Dk is ready the whole system integrates with it, updating itself with new information and results.

## Open research

Autonomous Health is joined to a research layer for biology and medicine, where researchers anywhere can share their studies and develop them with others, with partnerships with universities ([Open Science](https://science.drayker.org)). Volunteers can choose what is stored and processed on their own resources. Contributing that capacity earns the reward, the one transferable kind of reputation, which can give faster or priority access to resources.

## What is not decided

The order of the first steps, the clinical and legal requirements in each territory, and the criteria that make a probability "sufficient" for an investigation are open work. No clinic, dataset or diagnostic service exists today.
