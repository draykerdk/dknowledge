# Distributed Support

This is the current public formulation of Distributed Support in relation to the whole Drayker system.

## Systemic role

Support connects members, projects, communities and infrastructure to the capacity that lets them participate, learn, build and recover. Its human plane includes formation, tools, time, places, project resources, assistance under need and projects of life. Its infrastructure plane includes compute, storage, bandwidth, maintenance, redundancy and Stations.

A project of life relates a member's intentions, dreams, studies, experience, relationships and projects through Dk Personal, PAP and Dknowledge. It can be simple and local or highly ambitious; the system adapts to the person's direction rather than forcing one measure of ambition.

## Current economic interpretation

The internal economy is an economy of capacity, not a market among members. A future allocation can relate need, intention, contextual reputation, knowledge, trust, project quality, urgency, risk, expected impact and resource availability. Dk performs synthesis; Dknowledge preserves reasons and consequences; accountable member institutions authorize consequential allocations.

Synthesis alone does not authorize an action. Systemic safeguards — constitutional constraints, security, permissions, integrity, reserves and risk — can limit, postpone, compartmentalize or block what Dk proposes, so the executable decision is always the one inside a permitted and safe space. Effects, refusals and consequences return through members' personal Dks as new evidence, without a universal individual veto; a well-founded pattern of refusals can justify review, and a decision can be maintained, adapted, excepted, suspended or reversed.

Reputation matters under scarcity, risk and responsibility. It does not determine membership, dignity or fundamental rights. As capacity becomes abundant, essentials should move into a common floor rather than remain rewards.
