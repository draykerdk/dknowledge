# Dk Academy

First section written; the rest is open. See [Contributing](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md) to write it.

## Lessons, subjects and careers

Lessons form subjects, subjects form areas of knowledge, areas form specializations and specializations form careers, in Drayker and beyond, all of it flexible. Lessons are first made by Dk, from the most advanced material on each subject, and are updated whenever Dknowledge is. Each lesson keeps a space to go deeper, ask, correct errors and offer another explanation; an explanation that teaches better can become the main one, and the history stays available. Dknowledge's oracle turns the methods Dk itself creates into interactive lessons. None of this operates today.
