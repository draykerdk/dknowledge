# Will to potential

Will to Potential describes how automation can release people from work that neither develops them nor advances common capability, while learning, research, creation, care and participation become increasingly connected.

People remain constitutive of the intelligence: intentions give direction; experience gives context; relationships form communities; needs expose problems; creativity opens possibilities; projects turn intention into reality. Increasing member capability is therefore not a side effect of Drayker but one of its purposes.

Different trajectories are legitimate. A member may want a quiet life, improve a neighbourhood, care for others, build an enterprise, conduct science, create planetary infrastructure or explore space. Dk Personal adapts to that direction; it does not impose a single scale of ambition.
