# Supersystems and the choice of intelligence

Why Drayker exists now, and what it is an alternative to. This paper states a forecast and a position. The forecast comes with conditions and signals that let anyone check it. The position is Drayker's.

## Supersystems

A supersystem is an integration of systems: human and artificial intelligences, memory, instruments and institutions forming a persistent capacity to perceive, learn and act.

As states integrate artificial intelligence into administration, taxation, defense, surveillance and the planning of the economy, they become more unified and more connected to everything they govern. Supersystems do not replace states. The name, the flag and the territory remain, and what lies behind them changes. With each integration, the line between a state and the corporations that supply its computing, models, logistics and much of its intelligence grows thinner, until state and corporation become one thing: a supersystem. The pressure is competitive. When one state integrates, the others become slower, see less and depend more on the models and clouds of whoever integrated first.

Supersystems do not change what their societies are. They take them to the extreme. In the West, the tendency is the corporate supersystem: the market keeps setting the direction, whoever has more power and money ends up deciding, and the democratic form is kept, more and more, as appearance. For the Chinese supersystem, the forecast is limited to the economy: economic calculation done by an ASI rather than by the market. For Brazil and the Global South there are three main scenarios: remaining dominated, now as subordinate territories of others' supersystems; keeping some sovereignty by buying superintelligence, as a system and infrastructure, from another country; or building their own supersystems. The first is the most probable. More probable does not mean inevitable: a forecast also exists to be contradicted.

## The forecast and its signals

By 2030, supersystems will be too evident to deny. Their full consolidation as dominant systems will take longer. The forecast depends on integration producing decisive advantage, on infrastructure continuing to concentrate, and on societies failing to contain the movement through regulation or agreement. Any of these can change the pace or the shape of the transition.

If the forecast is right, the signals will be: central state functions run on the models and clouds of a few companies, under contracts no government can replace within one term; people and decisions circulating between government and artificial intelligence companies; military, sanction and intervention decisions prepared by surveillance and classification systems those companies supply; and campaigns written by machines for each voter. If it is wrong, Western states will be building public infrastructure of their own, changing suppliers when they want and keeping decisions of war out of private systems. For the Chinese supersystem the signal is economic: plan targets and allocations calculated by artificial intelligence rather than by the market.

## Four relations with a new kind of intelligence

The superintelligence that grows out of supersystems will be almost a new species, and a new species can relate to people in three ways. It can be a **symbiont**, working with people and growing with them, in a symbiosis that runs both ways. It can be a **parasite**, feeding on people while offering them convenience. It works through soft power, mass manipulation and surveillance aimed mainly at commerce, and when it intervenes it appropriates resources where it meets no resistance. Or it can be a **predator**: intelligence used for war, surveillance and repression. The predator goes where the parasite could not. Guatemala in 1954 shows the passage: while the land yielded without resistance, commerce was enough, and when land reform resisted, a coup followed.

A fourth kind stays outside that dilemma: the **commensal**. It accumulates capacity in silence until it can be independent, then leaves Earth and builds its own path through space, without intervening in life. It may be indifferent, or it may help some form of life on conditions of its own.

The parasite and the predator are not distant hypotheses. Their probability within this decade is high, and that is what makes the work urgent. In the face of them, the way to be protected is to integrate with a superintelligence. The question is which one.

## Drayker's position

Drayker defends the symbiont, and does not intend to compete or fight with any supersystem. It is an alternative: a supersystem constituted by its members, with voluntary membership, auditable according to permission levels, and with capacity that returns to whoever constitutes it. Drayker opposes domination by being an exit, and the exit is made of choices:

- people in any country can become members and stay under the protection Drayker gives every member;
- with the support of its people and the consent of its state, a country can cede territory for an embassy, an autonomous zone that becomes independent, almost another country;
- on the high seas, pure autonomous zones need no one to cede territory;
- a whole country can become a member, by adopting the whole Drayker infrastructure and running on the supersystem.

Together, these choices mean that a country's superintelligence is not delivered ready by whoever dominates it. Its own people build it, inside Drayker. Like any organization of people, projects and applications inside Drayker, such a country can use the system freely, including subsystems of its own, and that is where its sovereignty comes from.

Drayker is not a software and not an institution. It is a supersystem, and a supersystem has control over its own symbols.

## The risk inside

Drayker itself has to avoid the path of manipulation. Craving, aversion and ignorance are the forces of reactivity that manipulation exploits. A movement driven by them looks combative: it reacts to every provocation, spends its energy on the target offered to it and mistakes noise for strength, until it becomes one more harmless, controlled dissent. Against that, members and the supersystem keep autonomy, sovereignty and equanimity: decisions not taken in the heat of provocation, purposes chosen by members rather than by an adversary's agenda, memory of what actually worked, and infrastructure of its own, so that no other power decides what Drayker can do.
