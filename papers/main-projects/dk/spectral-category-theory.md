# Spectral category theory and functional ontologies

How Drayker proposes to unify low-level parallel computation, evolutionary code modulation, and living cultural ontologies under one mathematical and functional discipline. This paper synthesizes internal research notes connecting [Meta DFM](https://metadfmp.drayker.org), [BSDK](https://bsdk.drayker.org), and [Dknowledge](https://dknowledge.drayker.org). It is an open mathematical and architectural research proposal, not an implemented formalism.

## Extending category theory across parallel grades

Classical category theory studies mathematical structures (objects) and structure-preserving transformations (morphisms) within a well-defined space. Engineering an evolving, distributed intelligence introduces a second axis: the same conceptual function must simultaneously exist across **multiple parallel versions, hardware granularities, and confidence grades**, with explicit mappings governing how invariants hold or shift between them.

**Spectral category theory** names this research direction: extending categorical composition to families of **parallel structures evolving across multiple degrees** and the morphisms connecting versioned equivalents.

Inside DFM's biological-computational analogy:

- **Functions** act as minimal immutable structures — analogous to axons or discrete DNA segments — each transforming explicitly typed inputs into outputs with documented error boundaries, attack surfaces, and verification proofs.
- **Modules** act as regulatory structures — analogous to neuronal somas or epigenetic switches — coordinating when functions fire, allocating compute weights across hardware parallelism, enforcing effects boundaries, and propagating contextual updates without rewriting underlying primitives in place.
- **Evolutionary modulation** spawns parallel functional clones with adjustable weights matched to available parallel execution substrates (CPUs, GPUs, ASICs, and future quantum co-processors). Clones only displace predecessors after passing comparative tests (`D4`) and weakest-link trust gates.

## Symbolic networks and scale invariance

Human societies and distributed computing systems face the same structural challenge: coordinating parts across scale without erasing local diversity or losing auditability. Under Drayker's symbolic network model, DFM repeats the same organizational grammar fractally across levels:

- In **engineering**: minimal function → regulated module → subsystem piece → project codebase → distributed network (`BSDK` and `Dk Network`).
- In **collective coordination**: individual member (`Personal Dk` / vault) → working team or project (`PAP`) → distributed federation (`DAF`) → shared constitutional kernel → `Global Dk`.

At every scale, movement passes through the same **Expansion–Complexity–Harmony (E.C.H.)** dynamic: exposing new evidence or contradiction (**Expansion**), mapping dependencies and trade-offs (**Complexity**), and integrating coherent verified relations (**Harmony**).

Crucially, this transitions knowledge and architecture from an **unexamined regime** (implicit conventions, opaque heuristics, silent side effects) into a **consciously auditable regime** where dependencies, reasons, and historical revisions remain inspectable across versions.

## Objective ontologies coupled to subjective nuance

A rigid formal ontology breaks down as soon as it meets living human culture, divergent values, or shifting terminology. Conversely, purely statistical embeddings detach words from causal responsibility and provenance.

`Dknowledge` bridges both layers through spectral functional ontologies:

1. **Objective structural layer:** verifiable entities, causal events, functional dependencies, physical constraints, and decision provenance form a shared graph anchored in real-world observations and refutations.
2. **Subjective and cultural nuance clusters:** above and alongside objective relations sit weighted clusters representing how distinct communities, languages, or individuals experience, name, and prioritize concepts — capturing shared patterns analogous to a readable, inspectable collective memory without collapsing distinct viewpoints into one forced average.
3. **Lexical continuity:** when words evolve across decades or disciplines, new terms attach as topological neighbors and successor mappings while preserving historical decisions intact in their original wording.

At the lowest computational layer, cellular-automaton primitives inside [`BSDK`](./bsdk-dfm.md) execute deterministic local transitions at high concurrency while `Dknowledge` (System 1) and `Meta DFM` (System 2) govern which ontological structures persist, compress, or undergo revision.

## Open formal work

What remains open research is translating spectral composition laws into formal type theory and executable category checks capable of verifying invariant preservation across parallel `BSDK` mutations and multi-scale `Dknowledge` revisions.
