# Dk
Distributed K
Distributed Kernel
Distributed Knowledge

Intelligent data and network structure that supports intelligent algorithms and integrates them into a distributed core evolutionary super intelligence that supports the Drayker ecosystem.


Construction: https://github.com/draykerdk/DK


All proposed resolutions presented here are solutions to the requirements of Dk and Drayker platform and system, only those requirements are final. These motions for resolutions illustrate what should be done, but the definitive architecture will be structured around optimal solutions that are proposed and developed with metaprogramming intelligent algorithms and research organized through DFMP and other methods.

### Main Projects

The main projects are architectural syntheses and structural research motions forming initial baselines until `Dk` and `Meta DFM` assume continuous evolutionary refinement:

- [Dknowledge](./dknowledge.md) — memory that can be examined and corrected: source, use, correction and review of dependent conclusions.
- [Project and Application Platform (PAP)](./ap-platform.md) — liquid projects vs solid `DkApp` applications, pre-execution organizational simulation, five genesis modalities, anti-spam filter, and transitional absorption of `DAF`.
- [BSDK and DFM](./bsdk-dfm.md) — three kernel layers (mother equation, cellular automata, daughter backpropagation), immutability and fragment forgetting, Coupler/OSDK/Integrator triad, and falsificationist functional code.
- [Dk Network](./dk-network.md) — seven-layer topology, main vs secondary test network, islanded regeneration DNA, five node roles, 3/6 geographic replication, offline mesh, internal Red Team, and scarcity-weighted rewards.
- [Interface and Rayke](./interface.md) — voice/text to neural trajectory, ambiguity-free formal-expressive language, `Rayke` as living neural translator and second gateway, and speculative `Brainnet` cryptographic shroud continuity.
- [Living Cryptography](./living-cryptography.md) — research hypothesis coupling `uPOW` authentication to failure-triggered cryptographic mutation, Hydra DDoS inversion, and quantum compartmentalization.
- [OSDK](./osdk.md) — adaptive DFM VM to native OS/BIOS ladder, strict confinement of legacy external VMs, zero-driver hardware introspection, and sovereign 3-point local IoT.
- [UID and DkApp](./uid-dkapp.md) — living member operating environment, installed network applications, and sovereign identity boundaries.
