# Distributed Autonomous Federation (DAF)

How Drayker bootstraps real collaborative governance and resource stewardship *today* using existing tools — explicitly scoped as a transitional institutional form destined to dissolve into [`PAP`](../dk/ap-platform.md) once the core network operates. This paper synthesizes the [DAF](https://daf.drayker.org) design and states honestly what exists in Phase 0.

## Canonical definition and programmed dissolution

**DAF stands strictly for *Distributed Autonomous Federation*** (never *Decentralized*). It is a distributed federation of autonomous units — independent teams, laboratories, study groups, and partner organizations collaborating on distinct questions and build fronts across Drayker (conventional DAOs represent at most one optional sub-structure inside a unit, never the defining mold).

In institutional role, **DAF is the basic, primitive operational form of `PAP` in the present**: a way to test real-world coordination, accountability, and funding discipline *during the construction itself*. By constitutional design, DAF is temporary scaffolding: once `PAP`, `Dk`, and native distributed infrastructure operate reliably, federation records and contextually recalculated trust histories migrate to `PAP` and DAF dissolves.

## DFM tree coordination and last-resort financing

Rather than top-down corporate micromanagement or flat unstructured voting over raw treasuries, DAF structures execution as functional trees:

- **Three work initiation modes:** core modular tasks planned systematically by federation units, open-initiative proposals approved prior to funding, and retroactive reward claims submitted after independently verifiable delivery at the contributor's own initial risk.
- **Foundational functions and trust chains:** every autonomous unit organizes around a clear foundational function and proposes sequential module extensions. Unverified or negligent deliveries penalize the unit's trust rating for subsequent proposals, whereas modules reused reliably across peer units strengthen positive standing.
- **Rule of last resort:** funding operates strictly non-profit with maximum capital efficiency, unlocking external capital only where shared volunteer compute, stations, and capacity exchanges cannot bridge essential real-world costs.

## Transitional governance, assemblies, and founder safeguard

Until full network constitutional checks operate natively, DAF pairs explicit transparency with transitional anti-capture locks:

- Periodic public accountability cycles, non-transferable federative participation records, and quorum checks (explicitly marked transitional until full justified-veto and contextual triage replace numerical headcounts);
- **Grounded veto holds (`DAF-003`):** any well-substantiated objection backed by evidence halts integration pending open triage rather than being overruled silently;
- **Founding founder/embassy safeguard:** during the bootstrap phase, explicit safeguards protect against Sybil swarm capture or adversarial game-theoretic treasury attacks, programmed to dilute automatically as verified unit diversity and operational track records mature.

## Honest substrate evolution and Phase 0 reality today

Architectural notes across Drayker's archive trace an honest, non-speculative engineering progression:

1. **Early DAO sketches (preserved history, superseded):** initial organizational experiments explored generic web3 tooling (early Aragon/DAOhauS sketches), whose provisional accounting metaphors are now superseded.
2. **Phase 0 live today on GitHub (`draykerdk/dafp`):** public charter rules (`DAF-000` through `DAF-003`), deterministic CLI/event log helpers (`tools/daf.js`), and forum intake workflows are committed in git. **No external unit has registered and no formal assembly has been held yet.** Integration remains in founding bootstrap (`Hyadhuad`).
3. **Provisional transitional substrate (`ICP`):** should a funded pre-main-network compute stage open before `Dk Network` runs natively, Internet Computer canisters mirroring the event log byte-for-byte — potentially paired with hardware-attested trusted execution environments and conservative non-speculative liquidity custody — serve as the likely transitional execution bridge into [`Dktron`](../ecosystem/dktron.md). DAF carries **zero speculative token launch, ICO, or financial profit promise**.
