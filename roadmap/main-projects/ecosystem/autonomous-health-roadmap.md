# Autonomous Health — roadmap

The order in which [Autonomous Health](../../../papers/main-projects/ecosystem/autonomous-health.md) can be built. Each stage has an exit condition and no date. None of it operates today.

1. **A first clinic run with artificial intelligence.** A diagnostic intelligence and a treatment intelligence work in one clinic, with registered professionals and professional review. It can help finance the project while the rest is built. Exit: diagnoses and treatments whose results and feedback are recorded and improve the next ones.
2. **An open system for clinics.** Any clinic, hospital or organisation with an identity can use it, every professional is registered, and data and processing are distributed across the network. Exit: several independent clinics using the same system and the detection improving with each of them.
3. **Open research.** A shared space for biological and medical research, with university partnerships, where researchers share and develop studies together, and volunteers choose what is stored and processed on their resources. Exit: studies developed there by researchers who do not work together elsewhere.
4. **Integration with each member.** The system connects to each member's UID, Dk Personal, personal Dknowledge and devices, and investigates patterns that may be connected to a condition with sufficient probability. Exit: conditions detected earlier than they would have been, with the member's data under the member's control.
5. **Integration with Dk.** When Dk is ready, the whole system integrates with it and keeps updating itself with new information and results.
