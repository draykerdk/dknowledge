# Current orientation

This page separates Drayker's current public operating surfaces from its longer-term architecture and historical plans. It is intentionally not an internal roadmap, backlog or assignment board.

## Available now

- [`drayker.com`](https://drayker.com) presents the organization and the architecture at an institutional level.
- [`drayker.org`](https://drayker.org) maps the ecosystem and routes public participation.
- [`Dknowledger`](https://dknowledger.drayker.org) publishes the public knowledge base and its history.
- The [`General Forum`](https://github.com/draykerdk/general-forum/issues/new/choose) receives volunteer introductions, partnership proposals and questions that do not yet have a clear repository.
- Public repositories use issues, contribution branches, pull requests to `master`, automated checks and Git history.

## Architecture under development

Dk, BSDK, Dk Network, Living Cryptography, OSDK, UID, DFM/DFMP, Meta DFM, DAF, Advices, DFMPProject and the Emergence Initiative remain at different levels of documentation and specification. Their repository contracts in `.drayker/component.yml` state the evidence and limits for each component.

Dk Global, Dk Personal and Dk Local are connected architectural scopes, not three currently deployed products:

- **Dk Global** is the proposed shared, distributed scope.
- **Dk Personal** is the proposed user-controlled personal scope.
- **Dk Local** is the proposed local or organizational scope.

The precise interfaces and operational implementations among them are still to be specified and tested.

## Governance now

Drayker is in a founding phase. Public contribution is open, while the GitHub account [`Hyadhuad`](https://github.com/Hyadhuad) holds the explicit bootstrap authority to edit or integrate directly. Proposed DAF, DAO and council mechanisms are not presented as operating institutions.

The versioned governance source is [`draykerdk/.github`](https://github.com/draykerdk/.github).

## Reading historical material

Roadmaps and older papers remain public because they preserve the development of the ideas. They are not automatically current commitments, schedules, implementation evidence or promises of funding. Follow links to component repositories and inspect their current evidence contract before making an operational claim.
